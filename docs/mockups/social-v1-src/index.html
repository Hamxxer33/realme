Where Lovenest is different from Threads

Numbered pins show where each one lives in the screens above.

1

Following comes first

Chronological and the default. For you is optional and always says why.

2

Truly private messages

End-to-end encrypted. Requests stay text-only until you accept.

3

No ads, ever

Not in the feed, not between replies. Lovenest Plus pays the bills.

4

Edit any post

Fix it whenever you like. Everyone can see the edit history.

5

Hide like counts

On your posts, on everyone's, or both. Your call, one switch.

6

Time-aware by design

Quiet hours hold notifications, and feeds that end instead of scrolling forever.

7

No other account needed

Sign up with email, phone or a passkey. Nothing to link first.

8

Richer posts

Polls, multi-part posts, topics, reply controls, drafts and scheduling.

9

A supporter heart

Lovenest Plus shows a heart badge, never a check mark.

10

Your data, anytime

Export every post, reply and photo whenever you want.

Mockup — not final art. Free forever: posting, following, messaging. Optional Lovenest Plus for extras.
