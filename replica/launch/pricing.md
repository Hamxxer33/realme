## The model: free

Lovenest launches **free, with no ads, no in-app purchases and no
subscription**. The listing, the data safety form and the landing page
all say so. Reasons:

- The general messengers people already use are free. A paid messenger
  only works if everyone you talk to joins, and a paywall stops that.
- No billing means no Play Billing integration and no subscription policy
  review, and nothing to cancel or get refunded.
- Running costs are small at this stage: Railway, Neon and media storage,
  plus a TURN relay for calls.

## If money is needed later

At most one optional tier, for supporters, e.g. **Lovenest Plus**:
$2.99/month or $24.99/year (about 2 months free). It may add extras like
themes, app icons or larger media, but never touches the basics: messaging,
calls, groups and encryption stay free for everyone.

- On Android, digital goods must be sold through **Google Play Billing**,
  not Stripe. Play handles one-tap cancel, renewal reminders and refunds.
- Adding it means: update the listing ("In-app purchases: Yes"), the
  privacy policy (purchase history), and the data safety form.
- Stripe is only an option for something sold on the website.

No Stripe products to create now.
